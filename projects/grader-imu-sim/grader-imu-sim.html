How the automation works

The front wheel and the rear tandem bogie ride the real ground, so the mainframe pitches and heaves over every bump — and because the blade hangs off that frame, frame motion feeds straight into the cutting edge. A pass with the lift cylinders held fixed wanders about 27 cm; the automation holds roughly 2 cm.

Three two-state Kalman filters estimate mainframe pitch, drawbar pitch and cutting-edge elevation, each also solving for its sensor's unknown bias — watch the bias readouts converge to the true values in the first seconds. The controller sees only these estimates. Because the tandems ride the surface the blade just cut while the front wheel rides uncut ground, each pass genuinely smooths the next.
